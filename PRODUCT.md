# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Flint Hills residents seeking an honest investigation team, and readers who want to browse documented findings.

## Product Purpose

Offer free, confidential investigations and publish grounded field reports from the Flint Hills.

## Positioning

Local paranormal research presented like an evidence room: curious, methodical, and respectful.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: Request an investigation
- Secondary CTA: Browse evidence
- Remembered line: The unexplained deserves a careful record.
- Proof: Published findings, team profiles, equipment, and investigation reports.

## Brand Personality

Methodical, rural, uncanny. Dark but not theatrical; curious but not credulous.

## Anti-references

Avoid Halloween kitsch, haunted-house graphics, novelty horror copy, and generic startup layouts.

## Design Principles

- Evidence before spectacle.
- Local context should feel tangible.
- Make the archive easy to scan.
- Maintain an approachable, respectful voice.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Strong contrast, visible focus, semantic structure, reduced motion, and readable text at all sizes.
