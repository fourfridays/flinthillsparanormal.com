# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

People seeking help with unexplained activity in the Flint Hills and readers following the team's investigation reports.

## Product Purpose

Make it simple to request a respectful investigation and discover what the team has actually documented.

## Positioning

A field team for the unexplained, operating between local trust and late-night suspense.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: Request an investigation
- Secondary CTA: Read the latest finding
- Remembered line: Stay curious. Stay grounded.
- Proof: Findings archive, investigation services, team, equipment.

## Brand Personality

Urgent, grounded, cinematic. Thriller tension without sensationalism.

## Anti-references

No haunted attraction tropes, fake danger, paranormal clip-art, or generic dark SaaS dashboards.

## Design Principles

- A single clear action on every important page.
- Use tension as pacing, not as fake claims.
- Keep reports and people at the center.
- Make the local team feel reachable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect user zoom, keyboard navigation, motion preferences, semantic headings, and contrast.
