# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

People in and around the Flint Hills looking for a respectful paranormal investigation team, plus curious readers returning to explore published findings and evidence.

## Product Purpose

Flint Hills Paranormal offers free, confidential residential and business investigations, training, and referral services while publishing field findings in a way that is grounded, respectful, and easy to explore.

## Positioning

A local investigation team that treats strange encounters as fieldwork: careful observation, human respect, and an honest record of what was found.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: Request an investigation
- Secondary CTA: Read the findings
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: We investigate the unexplained without exploiting the people who live with it.
- Belief ladder: They are local and reachable; they take reports seriously; their methods are careful; their findings are worth returning to.
- Proof on hand: Published news and evidence pages, team biographies, equipment information, and first-hand investigation records.

## Brand Personality

Watchful, grounded, unsettling. The experience should feel like a late-night field notebook opened under a red flashlight: atmospheric but never campy, precise but never sterile.

## Anti-references

Avoid haunted-house kitsch, novelty Halloween graphics, generic horror-film posters, ghost emoji, fake paranormal claims, and a polished corporate non-profit look.

## Design Principles

- Show the record, not the spectacle.
- Make the local Flint Hills setting feel present.
- Let evidence and field notes lead the story.
- Keep the team approachable inside an eerie atmosphere.
- Respectful language is part of the investigation method.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic landmarks, visible keyboard focus, readable body text, reduced-motion alternatives, non-color-only cues, and strong contrast for all core content.
